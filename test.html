Before continuing the listening study, you must set up your headphones. Please go through the following steps:

  • Connect the headphones and ensure you have enough battery if you use Bluetooth headphones.
  • Wear the headphones, having the left can/earbud on the left hear and the right channel on the right hear.
  • Adjust the volume of the sound below to a comfortable level
  • Make sure that the level you have set is comfortable, as you should not change it from now on.
